# Tour

Chapter 1
Tour
Tutorial chapter - unlock traders and explore Tarkov's maps

## Story Context

"During the assault on the TerraGroup office, a massive explosion erupted on top of one of the nearby high-rises. I got left on my own, with no contact with command. Now I need to find my way through Tarkov and establish connections with the local traders."

## Required Items

- Roubles (Optional) ×250000
- Building materials ×5 (FIR)
- Weapon (any) ×2 (FIR)
- Dollars (Optional) ×8000
- PMC Dogtag (Optional) ×5

## Rewards

- Skier unlocked (trader)
- Mechanic unlocked (trader)
- Prapor unlocked (trader)
- Peacekeeper unlocked (trader)
- Access to 9 locations (map)
- Pathfinder (achievement)

## Objectives

During the assault on the TerraGroup office, a massive explosion erupted on top of one of the nearby high-rises. I got left on my own,
with no contact with command. Looks like I'll have to handle the evacuation myself.
But first things first, I have to get out of this Ground Zero.
Therapist said she can secure safe exit routes, but it's gonna cost. I'm not sure I can trust her, but there's no other option. I need to
gather the cash.
Therapist has provided me with safe routes through the city blocks.
I can now access streets of Tarkov when needed.
Ragman turned out to be a pretty reasonable person. He's not letting me through for free, but in exchange for a simple job, he's willing to
introduce me to one of the local big shots over by the customs terminal. That's something, at least.

Main objectives
☐ Survive and extract from Interchange or visit Interchange 3 times
☑ Talk to Therapist
☑ Hand over the cash to Therapist 250 000/250 000

Optional objectives
☐ Visit Interchange 0/3
☑ Collect the required amount in RUB 250 000/250 000

| Steps | Location | Description |
| - | - | - |
| Escape Ground Zero (tutorial raid) | Ground Zero | The tutorial raid. Extract via Klimov Street, behind the Skyside business center. None of the on-screen hints have to be followed. |
| Talk to Therapist || Through the trader menu. She is the only trader you start with. |
| Ensure access to Streets | Streets of Tarkov | Unlocked by the 250,000 roubles to Therapist, so this is the first location you open, not the last. |
| Hand over 250,000 Roubles to Therapist for Streets accessOptional || 250,000 roubles, handed over through dialogue. Does not need to be found in raid. This is what opens Streets of Tarkov. |
| Ensure access to Interchange | Interchange | Extract with Survived or Run Through, or simply visit three times. Completing it unlocks Skier as a trader and opens Customs. |
| Ensure access to Customs | Customs | Extract with Survived or Run Through, or visit three times. |
| Hand over 5 Building materials to Skier (found in raid) || Any 5 items from the building materials or tools category, and they must be found in raid. Partial handovers are not accepted, so carry all five at once. Unlocks Mechanic and Factory. |
| Ensure access to Factory | Factory | Extract with Survived or Run Through, or visit three times. |
| Hand over 2 Weapons to Mechanic (found in raid) || Any 2 weapons, found in raid. Unlocks Prapor and Woods. |
| Ensure access to Woods | Woods | Kill any 3 targets on Woods and extract with Survived or Run Through, or visit three times. The kills do not have to happen in the same raid as the extract. Unlocks Peacekeeper and Shoreline. |
| Interact with the intercom on Shoreline | Shoreline | South-east corner of Shoreline, at the watchtower in front of the Terminal. Do not approach the Terminal wall and do not shoot the guards, they return fire accurately. Pressing the intercom repeatedly after being told to leave also gets you killed. |
| Hand over 5 PMC dogtags to Prapor for Reserve accessOptional || Five PMC dogtags handed to Prapor. They do not need to be found in raid and you do not need to have killed the PMC yourself, so looting them off bodies counts. Unlocks Reserve. |
| Hand over $8,000 to Mechanic for Lighthouse accessOptional || 8,000 dollars, bought from Peacekeeper with roubles if you do not have them. Unlocks Lighthouse. |
| Locate and access The Lab facility | The Lab | Reached only by transit, using a TerraGroup Labs access keycard: either the underground entrance in front of Klimova 16A on Streets of Tarkov (the building with the cosmonaut painting), or the sewer tunnel off the flooded underground area on Factory. Transits only open one minute into a raid. |
| Search the top management offices in The Lab | The Lab | The laboratory manager's office (O21), on the second level. |
| Search the server room in The Lab | The Lab | On the first level, next to the hangar. |
| Locate the escape path through the drainage system in The Lab | The Lab | In the basement at the Sewage Conduit extraction. You do not have to activate the extract or survive the raid. Finishing this unlocks The Lab in location selection and the Pathfinder achievement. |
