# Chapter: Falling Skies

## Story Context


Mechanic and I got to talking about that plane that went down somewhere in the nature reserve. Nobody really knows what happened to it. I should track it down and take a look - maybe I'll find something interesting.

In the nature reserve, I came across a downed plane - or rather, what's left of it. I couldn't tell why it went down after looking at the wreckage. I should ask around with the traders, maybe someone knows more. I've got a strong feeling this plane is important.

Prapor doesn't trust me. He clearly won't be helping until I've earned his respect.

I've improved my standing with Prapor.
Now I can return to the matter of the downed plane.

Prapor sent me to find a flash drive in a G-Wagon.
Now I just need to figure out where exactly it is.
He said Therapist knows something about this.
I should pay her a visit.

Therapist's informant is asking for dollars in cash.
I need to bring them, unless I want to track down the vehicle on my own.

I gave the cash to Therapist.
She told me to wait a while.

Therapist's informant sent her a message: "Look for a black G-Wagon near the blown-out tunnel".

I found the flash drive.
Now I need to get it to Prapor.
Hopefully, he'll tell me what's on it later.

All done, the flash drive is now with Propor.
I'll have to wait until he's gone through all the data and calls me in.

The flash drive revealed that the plane in the nature reserve was shot down deliberately.
Prapor sent me to find the flight recorder.
It should help shed light on what really happened there.

I found the box.
Elektronik will decrypt it, but I need to leave it on the fishing island at the coast, in the corner room.

I left the flight recorder in place.
Now all that's left is to wait for the decryption results.
Hopefully, it'll be worth all this running around.

Prapor contacted me and called me over.
I didn't get any useful info, just another task.
But I have to complete it if I want to get to the truth.

He gave me a list of everything he needs.
I have to bring it all to him, otherwise Elektronik won't be able to decrypt the flight recorder.
