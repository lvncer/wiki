# The Ticket

Chapter 3
The Ticket
The main storyline leading to one of four possible endings

## Story Context

"After Falling Skies, Mr. Kerman contacts you about the armored case. This chapter leads to the four different endings based on your choices. Your path diverges based on whether you gave the case to Prapor or kept it for yourself."

## Required Items

- TerraGroup Blue Folders ×3 (FIR)
- Yellow signal flare ×1
- Experimental signal jammer (Optional) ×1 (FIR)
- Roubles (if refusing Kerman) (Optional) ×500000000

## Rewards

- Access to one of four endings, all of which escape through the Terminal finale (story)
- Savior - the full evidence route (achievement)
- Survivor - refuse Kerman and pay Prapor (achievement)
- Debtor - abandon the evidence route for Lightkeeper (achievement)
- Fallen - turn on Kerman and leave with Prapor (achievement)
